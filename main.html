About Me

I’ve always loved learning. I’m not quite sure who I am when I’m not discovering something new, pulling apart an idea, or wondering why something works the way it does. I’m fascinated not only by what people learn, but by how they come to understand it. For me, learning is curiosity, wonder, and that almost childlike feeling of suddenly seeing something differently.

Because learning hasn’t always come easily to me, I’ve spent years questioning the systems, methods, and environments that make understanding feel possible. I’m skeptical of learning models that ask people to copy patterns or memorize information without ever experiencing the reasoning behind them. I care about creating experiences that let people explore, make connections, and understand things in their own way — especially when the systems around them weren’t designed with them in mind.

That’s what draws me to learning and product design. I want to build experiences that work with how people think, feel, and learn rather than forcing them into rigid systems. I don’t just want educational products to be accessible or easy to use. I want them to preserve the magic of discovery — to make understanding feel rewarding, curiosity feel welcome, and knowledge feel like something that opens a door rather than another thing you’re simply expected to remember.
